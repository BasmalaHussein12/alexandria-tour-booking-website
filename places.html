Bibliotheca Alexandrina

The Bibliotheca Alexandrina is a major library and cultural center located on the shore of the Mediterranean Sea. It is both a commemoration of the Library of Alexandria that was lost in antiquity, and an attempt to rekindle something of the brilliance that this earlier center of study and erudition represented. The library features a reading room that covers 20,000 square meters across eleven cascading levels, and shelf space for eight million books.

Qaitbay Citadel

The Citadel of Qaitbay is a 15th-century defensive fortress located on the Mediterranean sea coast. It was established in 1477 AD by Sultan Al-Ashraf Sayf al-Din Qait Bay. The Citadel is situated on the eastern side of the northern tip of Pharos Island at the mouth of the Eastern Harbour. It was built on the exact site of the famous Lighthouse of Alexandria, which was one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.

📍 Eastern Harbour, Alexandria ⏰ Open 9AM - 5PM ★ 4.8 Rating

Montaza Palace

Montaza Palace is a palace and extensive gardens in the Montaza district of Alexandria. It was built as a hunting lodge in 1892 by Khedive Abbas II, the last Muhammad Ali Dynasty ruler to hold the Khedive title. The palace and its gardens cover an area of over 350 acres, featuring beautiful beaches, lush gardens, and a unique blend of Turkish and Florentine architectural styles. The Salamlek Palace now serves as a museum.

📍 Montaza District, Alexandria ⏰ Open 8AM - 6PM ★ 4.7 Rating

Alexandria Corniche

The Alexandria Corniche is the iconic waterfront promenade that stretches along the Mediterranean coast of Alexandria. It is one of the most beloved public spaces in Egypt, offering stunning sea views, fresh Mediterranean breezes, and a vibrant atmosphere. The Corniche connects many of Alexandria's famous landmarks, including the Citadel of Qaitbay, the Bibliotheca Alexandrina, and Stanley Bridge. It's the perfect place for evening walks.

📍 Alexandria Coastline ⏰ Open 24 Hours ★ 4.8 Rating
